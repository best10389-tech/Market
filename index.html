What do these mean?

Nifty 50 tracks the 50 biggest companies on the NSE. Sensex tracks 30 big companies on the BSE. Bank Nifty tracks the main banks. If they go up, the market is generally doing well. If they go down, it is generally weak.

A move of about 1% in a day is normal. A move of 1.5% or more is a big day.
